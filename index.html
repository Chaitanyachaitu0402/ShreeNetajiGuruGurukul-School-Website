About Us

The Remarkable Journey of Shree Netaji Gurudev Gurukul

Shree Netaji Gurudev Gurukul, located in Tandur, Telangana Vikarabad District, Telangana, was established in 2015–2016 with the vision of providing quality education and creating a strong foundation for the students of the region. The school was founded to make modern, value-based education accessible to every child while promoting academic excellence, discipline, and ethical values.

Since its inception, the school has been committed to nurturing young minds through a safe, inclusive, and student-friendly learning environment. Equipped with qualified teachers, modern teaching methods, and a balanced curriculum, the institution focuses on developing students academically, socially, and emotionally. Along with classroom learning, equal importance is given to sports, cultural activities, leadership, and life skills.

Today, Shree Netaji Gurudev Gurukul continues to grow as one of the trusted educational institutions in Tandur, Telangana Vikarabad District, Telangana. With the support of dedicated faculty, caring parents, and the local community, the school strives to shape confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals who are prepared to excel in higher education and contribute positively to society.
