Privacy Policy

Welcome to the official website of Shree Netaji Gurudev Gurukul. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share while visiting our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage information obtained through our website.

Information We Collect

Our website is primarily intended to provide information about our school, academic programs, admissions, events, and other educational activities.

We may collect limited personal information only when you voluntarily provide it through enquiry forms, admission forms, contact forms, or other communication channels available on our website.

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Student or Parent Enquiry Details

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for school related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Providing admission-related information.
  • Communicating important school announcements.
  • Improving our website and educational services.
  • Maintaining effective communication with parents and students.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and Google Analytics to better understand visitor interactions and improve website performance.

  • Cookies help improve browsing experience and website functionality.
  • Anonymous information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and session duration may be collected.
  • No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics services.

Data Protection

Shree Netaji Gurudev Gurukul takes appropriate technical and administrative measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

Although we strive to maintain the highest security standards, no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites such as Google Maps, Google Forms, social media platforms, or educational resources.

These external websites operate under their own privacy policies, and Shree Netaji Gurudev Gurukul is not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is extremely important to us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of their parents or guardians.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request removal of information you have submitted.
  • Contact the school regarding privacy-related concerns.
  • Receive clarification about how your information is used.

Updates to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in school practices, legal requirements, or website improvements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
